Originated here

These tools and ideas came from EleutherAI. Some went on to define the field.

LM Evaluation Harness
Evaluation

The standard benchmark suite for language models. Released in 2020, it became the de facto evaluation tool for the field — the same framework powering Hugging Face's Open LLM Leaderboard, and used by every major lab when comparing models.

Used by
Hugging Face Google Meta OpenAI Mistral AI2
Pythia
Training Interpretability

The first model suite with full training transparency. Published April 2023 — every checkpoint, every gradient, every data sample, in order. OLMo — widely credited for introducing open training transparency — came February 2024, eleven months later.

Used in research at
MIT Stanford DeepMind Berkeley CMU
YaRN
Architecture Efficiency

Yet Another RoPE extensioN — enabling 128K+ token context windows without retraining from scratch. The technique for extending context in transformer models, adopted by the teams building today's most capable frontier models.

Used in
DeepSeek Qwen Kimi K2 Mistral
Sparse Autoencoders
Interpretability

First arXiv paper on SAEs for mechanistic interpretability — September 2023, three weeks before Anthropic's "Towards Monosemanticity." EleutherAI established the technique; the field followed.

SAE weights released for
Pythia Llama 3 Llama 3.1 GPT-2

Used by interpretability researchers at Anthropic, DeepMind, and across academia.

The Pile
Datasets

825 GiB of diverse, documented training data — the first large-scale open training dataset with transparent source documentation. Set the standard for what an open training corpus should look like.

Trained on The Pile
GPT-NeoX-20B Pythia Falcon + many others
GPT-NeoX
Training Infrastructure

Open-source large-scale LLM training library. When GPT-3 was closed-source and frontier training was opaque, EleutherAI built and released the infrastructure to train at scale — freely, with full documentation.

Used to train
GPT-NeoX-20B Pythia OLMo Falcon
