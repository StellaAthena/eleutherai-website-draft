02

Make reported results interpretable.

Evaluation Cards and Every Eval Ever organize results that are otherwise scattered across papers, leaderboards, and model cards. They preserve provenance and expose whether evidence is complete, comparable, and reproducible.

Developed with the EvalEval Coalition. EleutherAI sponsors the coalition, and Stella Biderman serves on its steering committee.

03

Study the measurement instrument itself.

Benchmarks are not fixed rulers. Their usefulness changes as models improve, data leaks, and aggregate scores hide differences between tasks and groups.

Saturation

Benchmarks can stop distinguishing between capable models.

We measure when widely used evaluations plateau and what should replace them.

Resolution

Average scores can conceal the dynamics researchers care about.

Disaggregating benchmarks reveals learning patterns hidden by a single number.

Validity

Contamination and defective items can turn precision into false confidence.

We trace how exposure, item quality, and reporting choices change the conclusions an evaluation supports.

Coverage

Social-impact evaluations leave important questions and communities out.

We map what first- and third-party evaluations cover, and where independent measurement is still missing.
