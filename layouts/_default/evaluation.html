Measurement is part of the science.

Evaluations do not merely report model quality. They define what progress looks like, which failures remain visible, and which deployment claims appear justified. A result is only as trustworthy as the benchmark, implementation, reporting, and interpretation that produced it.

Our work spans that entire chain. We build open evaluation infrastructure, diagnose when benchmarks stop measuring what researchers think they measure, and develop reporting systems that preserve the context flattened away by a single score.

EvalEval Coalition

A research community developing scientifically grounded research and robust infrastructure for broader-impact evaluations.

EleutherAI sponsors the independent EvalEval Coalition, where EleutherAI researchers help lead work on evaluation reporting, benchmark quality, and the structure of the evaluation ecosystem. Stella Biderman serves on its steering committee.

What trustworthy evaluation requires

Reproducibility

Enough information to run the evaluation again and understand disagreements between reported results.

Validity

Evidence that the benchmark measures its intended construct rather than contamination, shortcuts, or incidental properties.

Interpretability

Results reported with the context, disaggregation, limitations, and provenance needed to support a defensible claim.

Coverage

Measurement that includes languages, communities, social impacts, and failure modes omitted by standard capability suites.
