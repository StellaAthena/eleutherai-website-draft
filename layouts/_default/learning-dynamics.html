The important facts are decided during training

What a model memorizes, which capabilities it acquires, and which mechanisms produce its behavior are settled while it trains. A final checkpoint reveals the outcome, but hides the process that produced it.

We treat the trajectory itself as an object of study. Intermediate checkpoints, repeated runs, known data order, and longitudinal evaluations let us watch behavior form—and test explanations against development rather than only against an endpoint.
