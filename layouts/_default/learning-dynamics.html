Learning dynamics

Models are not static artifacts. We study how capabilities, representations, memorization, and failures develop over the course of training—and how those trajectories can support prediction and intervention.

Read the research agenda View all {{ len $papers }} papers

Models are processes, not artifacts

Most interpretability research examines a finished model, frozen at the end of training. That can reveal what is present without explaining when it formed, which parts of the training process produced it, or whether the same account holds across runs and scales.

Learning dynamics treats the training trajectory as the object of study. Intermediate checkpoints, controlled data order, repeated runs, and longitudinal evaluations let explanations be tested against development rather than fitted only to an endpoint.
