Research priorities

Evaluation

Measure model behavior reliably, identify benchmark failures, and understand when downstream capabilities are predictable.

Interpretability over training

Study mechanisms as time-evolving phenomena across checkpoints, data, scale, and fine-tuning.

Open infrastructure

Build models, datasets, and tools that let independent researchers reproduce and extend the work.

Open-weight safety

Develop safety techniques that can be inspected, stress-tested, and improved by the broader research community.
