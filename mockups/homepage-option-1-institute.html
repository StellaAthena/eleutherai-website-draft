What we study

Our current research asks what foundation models learn, when their capabilities emerge, how to evaluate them reliably, and how open-weight systems can be studied safely.

01

Open infrastructure

Build and release the models, datasets, and tools needed for independent research.

02

Evaluations

Understand what benchmarks measure, where they fail, and how capabilities change with scale.

03

Interpretability over time

Study mechanisms as they emerge across training, checkpoints, data, and scale.

04

Open-weight safety

Develop interventions and analysis methods for models that researchers can inspect directly.
