EleutherAI's research agenda is about making AI systems legible, reproducible, and safer in a field where the most important research objects are often controlled by a small number of companies. We train and release models, datasets, evaluations, and tools so researchers outside frontier labs can reproduce results, criticize claims, measure behavior, and set research agendas rather than only consume finished systems.

Our technical work connects evaluations, interpretability over time, and open-weight safety. We study how model behavior emerges during training, how checkpoints and longitudinal evaluations can make those changes observable, and how that understanding can support interventions before dangerous capabilities are deeply acquired. For open-weight systems, safety has a different threat model: once weights are released, safeguards cannot depend on API monitoring or revocable access. We focus on safety methods that remain compatible with inspection, modification, and broad public use.
